Being straight with you

This won't win a big-city race for the hottest bottle. It's built for small-town hunting instead.

Big cities: this will be inefficient — be honest with yourself

In big-city metro areas, the hottest allocated bottles are frequently gone within hours of arriving, sometimes before the day's data even updates. A once-a-day check cannot win that race, full stop. If you live in a big city and only care about the hottest bottles, you should expect very few useful alerts. We'd rather tell you that up front than take your $4.99/month and have you feel misled.

Smaller towns: this is the sweet spot

Outside the metro areas, allocated bottles regularly sit on shelves for days. A daily check plus automatic area-filtering is genuinely enough to get you there before it's gone.

The problem

Right now, finding a rare bottle means remembering to check a state website by hand, searching each one individually, and scrolling through stores across the whole state to find one near you.

Easy to forget

The list updates daily, but nobody logs in every single morning to search five or ten different bottle names, one at a time.

Existing tools make you do the work

The state's own search tool and other free trackers have the same data we do — but they're search-it-yourself, statewide, with no way to filter to just your area automatically and no alerts at all.

What's different

PourAlert is the layer nobody built: it checks the same daily data for you, and only tells you when it matters.
