PourAlert Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-21

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your email address, phone number, zip code, and the specific products you ask us to watch for. That's the complete list — we don't collect location data beyond the zip code you give us, and we don't track your browsing.

How we use it

Your phone number is used only to send you text alerts about the specific products you've asked us to watch, when they're in stock within the radius you chose. Your zip code is used only to calculate distance to nearby liquor stores — it is not shared, sold, or used for any other purpose.

Text messaging terms

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive text message alerts from PourAlert related to the products you're watching. Message frequency varies based on your watch list and how often matching products restock. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. We honor opt-out requests immediately and do not send further messages to a number that has opted out.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information with third parties.
  • We don't send marketing messages unrelated to the products you're watching.
  • We don't use your data to train any AI model or share it with data brokers.

Data we pull from public sources

PourAlert's product and store availability data comes from state alcohol control agencies' public inventory systems. We are an independent service and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@pouralert.com.
